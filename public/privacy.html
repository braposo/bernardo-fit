Privacy

This site is Bernardo Raposo's personal job search tool. It is not a public service, and the only Google account it connects to is Bernardo's own.

Gmail access

  • The tool uses read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) once a day to find replies from companies about job applications.
  • It reads the sender, subject, date and text of recent messages to suggest status updates, such as an interview invite or a rejection. Bernardo approves every change.
  • It never sends, deletes, labels or changes email.
  • Message text is sent to an AI model only to classify the message, and is not kept afterwards. The tool keeps the message ID, so it doesn't read the same email twice, and the short suggested update.
  • Gmail data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Visitors

Public pages (the CV and role fit pages) don't use tracking cookies or collect personal data beyond standard hosting logs.

Questions or deletion requests: braposo@gmail.com. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions.
